# ProofPass — Signal Ledger

## Theme name
Signal Ledger — original ZIP palette

## Intro
A crisp, welcoming student workspace for understanding a portable skill passport without exposing the underlying record. Match the supplied ZIP's forest-green, bright-lime, warm-paper, ochre, pale-blue, and clay palette, with its orbital credential illustration. Keep all current student and recruiter demo flows, and be direct that data and checks are simulated.

## Design movement
Trust infrastructure made human: evergreen structure, a lively lime accent, warm cream surfaces, and small touches of ochre, pale blue, and soft clay for category/status. Restore the original ZIP's credential/passport illustration with orbital rings and a compact credential chip. Keep small decorative/status dots removed; use purposeful graphic shapes instead.

## Core principles
- Make the 12-step journey legible and understandable at a glance.
- Treat privacy as a visible product behavior, not a footnote.
- Prefer evidence cards and readable labels, supported by a few deliberate illustrations.
- Clearly distinguish demo data and simulated claims from live verification.
- Keep the UI componentized and the frontend/backend contract explicit.
- Put a “How to use” guide at the top of the experience and in the main navigation.
- Show the accuracy meter's formula and underlying counts; never present sample evidence coverage as real-world model accuracy.
- Keep the separate sample recruiter portal available at `/employer`.

## Color philosophy
Use deep forest `#13231f` as the anchor, warm cream `#f4f2ec` and paper `#fbfaf6` for breathing room, bright lime `#b7f36b` for positive actions, and original muted green `#4d7136` for links. Reserve ochre `#f1df86`, pale blue `#bcd8ee`, and soft clay `#f3c6b6` for category/status accents. Avoid the previous cold navy/cobalt theme. Avoid relying on color alone to convey status.

## Layout paradigm
A responsive workspace with a compact desktop rail, a wide content canvas, and a mobile-first single-column flow. Keep the how-to guide discoverable at the top, followed by a focused status/summary area and purpose-built areas for workflow, credentials, insights, roadmap, student-controlled consent, and the separate recruiter demo. Avoid horizontal overflow at narrow widths.

## Signature elements
Use the original ZIP's ProofPass mark, an orbital credential illustration with a fingerprint chip in the hero, block-readable evidence summaries, monospaced DID/credential snippets, a computed profile-to-evidence meter, and a “what is shared / what stays private” disclosure block. Do not reintroduce the small decorative/status dots that were removed.

## Interaction philosophy
Every visible control has a clear purpose and state. Demo states are explicit. Consent actions explain their consequence and show success or decline feedback. The UI does not pretend to call real AI, Reclaim/zkTLS, a wallet, a Solidity contract, or an on-chain registry. Keyboard navigation, focus visibility, semantic headings, and accessible labels are first-class.

## Animation
Use only short, purposeful transitions for active navigation or state changes. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no cursor-following, scroll-triggered spectacle, or animation-dependent content.

## Typography system
Use a readable system sans-serif stack for interface copy and a system monospace stack for identifiers, claims, and verification records. Do not make the page depend on a third-party font request.

## Brand essence
Quiet confidence: a proof can be rigorous without being intimidating.

## Brand voice
Specific, transparent, and calm. Say what is verified in this demo, what is simulated, and what would be shared by a future real implementation. Do not invent metrics, customers, testimonials, or legal assurances.

## Wordmark/logo
Keep the original ZIP-provided ProofPass mark; do not substitute a newly invented symbol. Use the source SVG for the site mark and favicon, with the same full-bleed treatment for the project icon.

## Signature brand colors
Forest `#13231f`, lime `#b7f36b`, cream `#f4f2ec`, warm paper `#fbfaf6`, ochre `#f1df86`, pale blue `#bcd8ee`, and soft clay `#f3c6b6`.
